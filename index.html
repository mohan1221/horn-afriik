Common questions

Can I study offline?

Yes. After the first visit, lessons, tests and exams all work without internet. When you are signed in, your scores are saved to your account and sync when you are back online.

What is the pass mark?

A quiz counts as passed at 50% or higher. Your results screen shows it in green.

How is the streak counted?

Finish at least one quiz a day to keep your streak going.
